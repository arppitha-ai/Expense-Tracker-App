## Recommendations

- Set a festive-season shopping cap and use a 48-hour wait rule for purchases above Rs 2,000.
- Food budget is breached often: plan weekly groceries and cut delivery orders.
- Some transactions have no category; require a category at entry for cleaner reports.
- Create an emergency fund so one-off costs (medical, repairs) do not wipe out a month's savings.
- Review recurring subscriptions every quarter and cancel unused ones.
